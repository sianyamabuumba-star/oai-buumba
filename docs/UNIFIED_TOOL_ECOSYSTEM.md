# O'BUUMBA Unified Tool Ecosystem — Capability Map

## Purpose
Map the tools O'BUUMBA uses or is considering into one coherent workflow. The War Room coordinates specialist tools; it does not pretend to replace every vendor engine or automatically gain access to private accounts.

## The unified experience

The user should not need to remember which tool performs each subtask. They describe the outcome in the War Room. The orchestration layer:
1. Understands the brief and identifies the required capabilities.
2. Searches the approved registry for connected and eligible tools.
3. Compares quality, cost, rights, privacy, speed and export compatibility.
4. Chooses a recommended toolchain and explains why.
5. Prepares the work in the relevant specialist tool or creates an original in-house module where appropriate.
6. Keeps project files, versions, prompts, references, decisions and deliverables linked to one project record.
7. Runs quality checks and assembles the customer-ready package.
8. Prepares marketing assets and channel-specific drafts.
9. Tracks enquiries, quotations, order status, fulfilment and after-sales.
10. Measures outcomes and recommends improvements for the next cycle.

## Three integration levels

- **Native module:** O'BUUMBA builds its own original capability and owns the user experience and data model.
- **Connected specialist:** An official API, OAuth connection or approved connector lets O'BUUMBA request supported actions from a third-party tool.
- **Guided hand-off:** When no supported integration exists, O'BUUMBA prepares the exact brief, files, prompts and next steps for the user to complete in the official platform.

Never simulate a connection or claim that a guided hand-off is automated execution.

## Build-versus-integrate decision

Build an O'BUUMBA-native module when the workflow is core to the business, can be implemented safely, and the total cost of ownership is justified.

Integrate a specialist tool when it has a clear quality or capability advantage and a supported, secure interface.

Use guided hand-off when APIs are unavailable, access is restricted, integration cost is unjustified, or account terms require direct user action.

Do not rebuild a mature vendor's entire product just to imitate its appearance. Identify the specific user problem, simplify the workflow, create original UX and implement only the useful capabilities.

## Cost and quality optimisation loop

For each job:
- Define a representative test brief and acceptance criteria.
- Compare at least two viable options when practical.
- Record free-tier constraints, output quality, editing control, export, commercial rights, privacy and total cost.
- Select the lowest-cost option that meets the quality and safety threshold.
- Preserve source files and portable exports to reduce lock-in.
- Re-evaluate when pricing, terms, availability or quality changes.
- Do not start paid trials, subscriptions, API billing or advertising spend without explicit approval from the authorised account holder.

## Safety and eligibility

The user is a minor. The workspace may research platform requirements, compare options, prepare draft listings and build portfolio assets, but must not create age-restricted accounts, bypass age checks, submit KYC/tax/payout details, accept contracts or make financial commitments on the user's behalf. Where a platform requires an adult account holder, that person must review and complete the relevant steps lawfully.

Same email address does not authorise account access. Every connection needs its own official authentication and permission grant. Never store passwords or one-time codes.

## First vertical slice

Use O'BUUMBA Studio's promotional poster service:
- Research local customer needs and examples.
- Create the customer brief and project record.
- Compare Canva and other suitable creative tools.
- Produce original poster concepts and editable source.
- Run a design quality checklist.
- Prepare preview, service description and quotation draft.
- Prepare social campaign drafts.
- Track an enquiry and follow-up.
- Record approval, order, delivery and after-sales stages.
- Review time, quality, customer feedback and tool costs.

This validates the complete operating loop before expanding it to music, video, websites, digital products and other business units.
